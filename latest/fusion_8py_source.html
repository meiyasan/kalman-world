1"""
2``add_sensor``: a model read by one more sensor.
3
4The Python face of ``Kalman::Models::Fusion`` (``include/Kalman/Models/Fusion.h``):
5the state and its dynamics are the inner model's, and the measurement grows by
6the channels of a :class:`~kalman.framework.sensor.KalmanSensor`,
7
8 z = [ h_inner(x, t) ; g(x, t) ] R = diag(R_inner, R_sensor)
9
10One filter update then weighs every sensor by its noise: that is sensor fusion.
11A model of matrices with a matrix sensor added is still a model of matrices.
12Nothing is computed here.
13
14Three ways to the same model::
15
16 model.add_sensor(sensor)
17 add_sensor(model, sensor)
18 Fusion(model, sensor)
19
20This is not ``compose``, which puts models with their own states side by side:
21here a second instrument reads the same state.
22"""
