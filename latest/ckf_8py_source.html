8class CombinatorialKalmanFilter(KalmanFilter):
9 """
10 Combinatorial Kalman filter: track *finding* rather than track following.
11
12 Where KF/EKF/UKF/PF estimate **one** trajectory from a time series of single
13 measurements, the CKF reconstructs **several tracks** from an event whose every
14 layer holds **many hits** (signal + background)::
15
16 model = DriftChamberModel(n_layers=8, Bz=0.05)
17 event = model.generate_event(n_tracks=3, n_background=4, seed=0)
18 ckf = CombinatorialKalmanFilter(model)
19 tracks = ckf.forward(event.hits, layer_radii=event.radii) # KalmanRecordCollection
20 tracks = ckf.backward() # RTS-smoothed
21
22 The search — seeding, χ² gating, branching, beam pruning, hit arbitration and the
23 smoother over the found tracks — is the algorithm, so it lives in
24 :class:`~kalman.framework.engines.combinatorial.CombinatorialKalmanEngine`, which
25 declares ``OWNS_PASS`` and drives the pass itself. This class only names that engine
26 and hands it the search hyper-parameters, exactly as ``UnscentedKalmanFilter`` hands
27 ``alpha/beta/kappa`` to its own engine.
28 """
29
