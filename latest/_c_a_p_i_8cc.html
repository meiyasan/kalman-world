Find tracks in one event with Kalman::Engines::Combinatorial.

radii holds the n_layers layer radii, innermost first; hits_per_layer the number of hits on each; hits every hit, layer after layer, m values each (NaN marks a missing channel). The n_seeds seeds are given explicitly: the layer and index of the hit each starts from, its state seed_x (n each) and covariance seed_P (n x n each). chi2_cut and hole_penalty may be NaN to take the defaults (99% of a chi-square with m degrees of freedom; one gate). Returns the arbitrated tracks, best first, or NULL with kalman_last_error().
