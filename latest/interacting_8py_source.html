1"""Interacting engine: the predict/update step of a model with several modes.
2
3The Python face of ``Kalman::Engines::Interacting``
4(``include/Kalman/Engines/Interacting.h``), the Interacting Multiple Model
5filter of the literature. It runs a model made by ``route``
6(:class:`~kalman.framework.models.route.Router`): one Gaussian belief per mode
7and a probability on each; at every step the beliefs are mixed, each mode
8predicts and updates with its own engine, the decision layer gives the new
9probabilities, and the estimate is the modes' combined.
10
11The whole pass runs in C++ (``Kalman::Filter``); nothing is computed here. After
12a pass the engine holds what the C++ engine recorded: ``mode_probabilities``
13and ``nis_history``, each ``(T, M)``.
14
15Words of this field, kept here: *mode*, *mixing*, *mode probability*.
16"""
