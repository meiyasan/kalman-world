Detailed Description

One track hypothesis under construction — a node of the search tree.

Carries the current posterior, the accumulated chi-square and score, and, per assigned hit, everything the smoother later needs: the posterior, the prediction it corrected, the hit, the radius, and the transition product since the previous assigned hit (holes fold into it).
