Detailed Description

A set of sensors, and what they share.

A sensor is one reading of the state. An instrument is the piece of hardware that carries one or more of them: a weather station (temperature, CO2 and humidity on one board), a receiver, a camera. What its sensors share is the instrument's:

  • a name, under which its sensors are grouped (Model::Instruments());
  • a rate, for the sensors that state none of their own;
  • a calibration: every reading goes through one gain and one offset,
z = gain * g(x, t) + offset

which become two named parameters of the model that carries the instrument, <name>.gain and <name>.offset. Being parameters, they can be estimated along with the state (Models::Joint): that is calibrating the instrument while it measures. Whether the data can tell an offset from what is measured is what Kalman::Observability answers; a second instrument, already calibrated, usually settles it.

An instrument is made by composing sensors, the way a composite model is made by composing models: Kalman::Compose({s1, s2}, "station"), or s1 | s2. Model::AddInstrument() then gives the model read by all of them.
