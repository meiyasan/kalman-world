7class BayesianRouter(KalmanRouter):
8 """Classical IKF update: μ_j ∝ c_j · L_j.
9
10 Combines the Markov transition prior ``c`` with the Gaussian innovation
11 likelihood ``L_j = N(ν_j; 0, S_j)``. Theoretically optimal when all
12 models are correctly specified Gaussians.
13
14 No training required — ``fit()`` is a no-op.
15
16 Prefer over NeuralRouter when
17 - noise is truly Gaussian and models are correctly specified;
18 - training data is scarce;
19 - transitions are genuinely Markov (no learned pattern adds value);
20 - interpretability matters (every μ update follows Bayes' theorem).
21
22 Decided by ``Kalman::Routers::Bayesian`` when libKalman is installed;
23 :meth:`_reference_route` is the Python reference.
24 """
