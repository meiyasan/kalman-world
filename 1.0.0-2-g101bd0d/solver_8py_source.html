2"""
3Abstract **solver** base — the spatial-discretization strategy.
4
5A solver turns a continuous field problem into something an integrator can march
6in time. Contract:
7
8 - ``discretize(field)`` — the solver's own job: form the semi-discrete RHS
9 (spatial part), e.g. the pseudo-spectral RHS for a PDE;
10 - ``integrate(...)`` — the *time* march, **delegated to a composed**
11 :class:`~kalman.framework.integrator.KalmanIntegrator` (one home for the
12 stepping math);
13 - ``solve(field, steps, u, t)`` — the public entry: discretize then
14 integrate over the interval.
15
16For an ODE ``discretize`` is the identity, so ``solve`` ≡ ``integrate`` — which
17is why "solver ≈ integrator" for ODEs. **Composition, not inheritance**: a
18solver *has-a* integrator (different solvers pair with different integrators).
19
20A tensor **backend** (``"numpy"`` default, ``"torch"`` optional) with a
21``device`` (``"cpu"/"cuda"/"mps"``) lets field solvers run on GPU/MPS without any
22per-model change; concrete solvers honour it where it pays (see
23``solvers/spectral.py``).
24"""

67 """Public entry: ``discretize`` → ``integrate`` → return the evolved field.

69 raise NotImplementedError
