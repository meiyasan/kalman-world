Detailed Description

Several records handled as one — the output of a multi-target pass.

The counterpart of KalmanRecordCollection. One filtered trajectory is a Kalman::IO::Record; a track finder, a multi-target tracker or a bank of per-regime filters produces many, and they travel together.

It is a thin sequence rather than a container with opinions: iteration, indexing and a summary line. What it adds over a bare std::vector<Record> is a name and a place to hang collection-level reporting, so callers do not each invent their own.
