1"""
2``route``: several models of one system, with a probability on each.
3
4The Python face of ``Kalman::Models::Router`` (``include/Kalman/Models/Router.h``).
5A system that changes regime (a car on a dry road, then on ice; a storm that
6runs straight, then recurves) is described by one model per regime, the
7*modes*. They share the state and the measurement; a Markov matrix says how
8likely the system is to go from one to another in a step, and the model
9carries a probability on each.
10
11Three ways to the same model::
12
13 route([dry, icy], Pi)
14 KalmanModel.route([dry, icy], Pi)
15 Router([dry, icy], Pi, initial_probs, router=ThresholdRouter())
16
17 record = modes.filter(zk) # the combined estimate
18 modes.mode_probabilities # (T, M): which regime, when
19
20It is run by the interacting engine (``Kalman::Engines::Interacting``, the
21Interacting Multiple Model filter of the literature), its default engine, by
22the ordinary filter: there is no filter class of its own. Who decides the
23probabilities at each step is the ``router=``, a decision layer from
24``kalman.framework.routers`` (Bayesian by default; a rule, a network, a
25blend), or any function ``(nis, c, likelihoods) -> mu``.
26
27Seen as one model it answers ``f``, ``h`` and its matrices with those of its
28leading mode, the most probable one. Nothing is computed here.
29"""
