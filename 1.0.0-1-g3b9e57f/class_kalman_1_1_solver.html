Detailed Description

The spatial discretization — HOW, in space.

A solver turns a continuous field problem into something an integrator can march in time. Its own job is the spatial part — the pseudo-spectral right-hand side, the finite-difference stencils; the time march is delegated to a composed Kalman::Integrator, one home for the stepping arithmetic. For an ODE the discretization is the identity and a solver is an integrator, which is why an ODE model binds an integrator directly and a PDE model binds a solver.

Composition, not inheritance: a solver has an integrator, and different solvers may pair with different integrators. Mirrors modules/framework/solver.py.
