Detailed Description

The 2-D vorticity equation on the spectral solver.

Kalman::Solvers::Spectral ships no physics. This subclass is the equation the framework's flow models share,

dw/dt = -u.grad(w) - beta v + nu lap(w) + F

on a periodic grid: derivatives and the velocity in Fourier space, the products on the grid, 2/3-rule dealiasing. With beta = 0 and a forcing it is the Kolmogorov flow; with beta and no forcing, the barotropic beta-plane.

It steps one field at a time with the built-in FFT and needs nothing outside libKalman. Kalman::Learn::Solvers::Vorticity is the same equation on libTorch, batched, for CPU and GPU.
