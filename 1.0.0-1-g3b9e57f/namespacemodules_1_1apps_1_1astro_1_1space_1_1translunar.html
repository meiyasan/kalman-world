Third-body perturbation in a frame centred on the primary: ``GM[(r_b-r)/|r_b-r|³ −
r_b/|r_b|³]``. The first term is the body's direct pull on the spacecraft; the second
(indirect) subtracts its pull on the central body, so a distant body contributes only its
true (tiny) tidal effect rather than a spurious bulk force.

108def gravity(r, t, moon_phase=0.0, sun_phase=0.0, bodies=("earth", "moon", "sun")):
109 """Geocentric acceleration on a spacecraft at ``r`` (scaled): Earth's central pull plus
110 the **third-body perturbations** of the Moon and Sun (analytic circular orbits)."""
111 r = np.asarray(r, dtype=float).reshape(3)
112 a = np.zeros(3)
113 if "earth" in bodies:
