8class MPCKalmanController(KalmanController):
9 """Plans ``horizon`` commands ahead at every step, honouring limits on the
10 command while it plans, applies the first one, and plans again.
11
12 The plan minimises ``Σ eᵀ Qcost e + uᵀ Rcost u`` over the horizon, with a
13 terminal cost (the Riccati solution, so that with no limit in the way the
14 command is the LQR command), subject to ``u_min ≤ u ≤ u_max`` and, when
15 given, ``|u_k − u_{k−1}| ≤ du_max``. The prediction is the model's own ``f``:
16 exact for a model of matrices, linearised along the previous plan otherwise
17 (``n_iterations`` passes per step).
18
19 Beside the others: LQR is one gain and knows no limit; MPPI replans by
20 rolling random command sequences through ``f``. This one replans
21 deterministically, and the limits are constraints of the plan.
22
23 The planner is ``Kalman::Controllers::MPC`` (``include/Kalman/Controllers/MPC.h``);
24 this class forwards to it and has no implementation of its own, so it needs
25 libKalman.
26
27 Parameters
28 ----------
29 horizon : steps planned ahead.
30 u_min, u_max : limits on the command (a number, or one per command); ``None`` for none.
31 du_max : limit on its change per step; ``None`` for none.
32 n_iterations : linearisation passes per step (1 is enough once the plan is warm).
33 """
