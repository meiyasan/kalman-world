Detailed Description

Barotropic vorticity on a mid-latitude beta-plane — the twin of kalman.apps.weather.BarotropicFlowModel.

The field model of Weather::KolmogorovFlow with the physics of the beta-plane: beta = 2 Omega cos(lat) / a, no forcing, a rectangular box in metres, SI units. The defaults are the Python class's: 64 x 64 over a 20 x 20 degree window at 50 N (2,224 km by 1,430 km), nu = 2e4 m^2/s, 60 steps of 60 s per cycle. The parameters and arrays are KolmogorovFlow's.
