Detailed Description

Everything about a gate that depends on the prediction only, not on the hit.

At one layer a candidate is tested against every hit. The predicted measurement, its Jacobian, the innovation covariance, the gain and — since the Joseph form never mentions z — the posterior covariance itself are identical for all of them, so they are computed once per (candidate, layer).
