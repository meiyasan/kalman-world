Detailed Description

Closed-loop control over a Kalman filter — u = ctrl(x_hat, P, t, nu).

A controller is what a Kalman::Filter calls once per step, in place of a recorded control sequence, to decide the command from the current belief: the posterior mean and covariance, the time, and the last innovation. It may also adapt the model as a side effect — a gain boost scales R, an adaptive scheme scales Q — through Update(), which runs after Policy().

Every call logs the quadratic cost e^T Qcost e + u^T Rcost u with e = x_hat - x_ref, charged against the command the actuator can actually deliver (Kalman::Model::ClipControl), not the controller's raw ask: a fixed-gain controller with no notion of an actuator limit can ask for far more than the plant ever receives, and costing that fictitious effort would inflate it against a controller whose output already respects the limit.

Mirrors modules/framework/control.py; the concrete policies live in Kalman/Controllers/.
