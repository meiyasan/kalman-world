Detailed Description

Small dense linear-algebra helpers shared by the engines.

Author
Marco Meyer <marco.nosp@m..mey.nosp@m.er@om.nosp@m.u.ac.nosp@m..jp>

The C++ counterpart of the numeric helpers in modules/helpers.py and modules/framework/native/kalman.c. Everything here is a free function over Kalman::Vector / Kalman::Matrix: no state, no allocation policy, nothing that belongs to a model or an engine.

Two conventions are load-bearing and shared with the Python framework:

  • NaN marks a missing measurement channel. ValidChannels() reports the channels that are present, and the Compress* helpers cut z/H/R down to them, so a partially-blind sensor costs no special case upstream.
  • Symmetric inverses degrade instead of failing. Inverse() tries Cholesky, retries with a growing Tikhonov ridge, and finally falls back to an eigenvalue pseudo-inverse. A covariance that has drifted slightly non-positive-definite must not abort a run.
