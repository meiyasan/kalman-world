9 """Linear Quadratic Regulator: ``u = −K · (x̂ − x_ref)``.
10
11 The gain ``K`` is computed from the Discrete Algebraic Riccati Equation —
12 the analytically optimal solution for linear systems — for the model's
13 ``F()`` and ``B()``. Call ``fit()`` before use.
14
15 With libKalman installed the equation is solved and the policy computed by
16 ``Kalman::Controllers::LQR`` (``Kalman::Algebra::SolveDARE``, which agrees
17 with scipy's ``solve_discrete_are`` to rounding); scipy is the reference.
18 """
