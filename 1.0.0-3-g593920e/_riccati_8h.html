Detailed Description

The discrete algebraic Riccati equation, and the Stein equation it is refined with.

Author
Marco Meyer <marco.nosp@m..mey.nosp@m.er@om.nosp@m.u.ac.nosp@m..jp>

What a linear-quadratic regulator needs: the stabilising solution of

P = Q + A^T P A - A^T P B (R + B^T P B)^-1 B^T P A

Solved without a general factorisation: the Riccati recursion is iterated until its gain stabilises the pair (its own convergence is linear, and slow when the closed loop has a pole near the unit circle), then Hewer's Newton step takes over: for the gain K of the current P, the cost of that gain is the solution of the Stein equation X = (A - B K)^T X (A - B K) + Q + K^T R K, which converges quadratically. The Stein equation is summed by doubling, with matrix products only. The one inverse is of R + B^T P B, a covariance- like SPD matrix, through Cholesky. Agrees with scipy's Schur-based solve_discrete_are to rounding (docs/15 has the measurements).
